01 / ABOUT

Industrial rigor meets research depth.

For three years at Accenture, I delivered structural engineering for Airbus aircraft programs in Toulouse — FEA of fuselage sections, composite sizing, GFEM integration and the Torquebox door-frame concept for future A320 programs.

Before that, my Ph.D. at I2M Bordeaux developed hybrid AI–physics methods for nonlinear multiscale simulation. The resulting FE-LSTM approach earned the CSMA PhD Thesis Award 2022 (selected among 28 theses) and an ECCOMAS award nomination.

Aerospace structures · Airbus01

Torquebox door-frame concept for A320 fuselage

PROBLEM
Design a new composite door-frame concept for the A320 nose fuselage and section S14A while meeting multiple composite sizing criteria.
METHOD
GFEM integration, model condensation and stress analysis in HyperMesh / MSC Nastran; iterative sizing and mass optimization.
RESULT
≈10% overall mass reduction on the door-frame, supporting future A320 programs.
HyperMeshMSC NastranGFEMComposites
Computational mechanics · PhD02

FE-LSTM: deep learning inside nonlinear FEA

PROBLEM
Nonlinear multiscale simulations of heterogeneous and architected materials require HPC and days of compute.
METHOD
Hybrid physics–AI approach: LSTM networks trained on micro-scale responses embedded as constitutive models in the FE framework.
RESULT
Up to 99% computational time reduction — HPC-class simulations running on a standard desktop.
PythonTensorFlowKerasSimcoonFedoo
