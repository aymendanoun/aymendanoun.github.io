01 / ABOUT

Mechanics Meets Intelligence

I’m Aymen Danoun, a Mechanical and Aerospace Engineer with a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering. I combine hands-on experience in aircraft structural analysis with award-winning research in hybrid AI–physics modeling.

Over three years supporting Airbus aircraft programs, I worked with multidisciplinary teams on stress analysis, composite sizing, and structural development. This experience strengthened my ability to translate design requirements into practical analysis and adapt models as aircraft designs evolve.

During my Ph.D. at I2M Bordeaux, I combined artificial intelligence with computational mechanics to make complex material simulations faster and more accessible, achieving up to 99% less computation time. My work connected the development of new methods with their application to structural problems.

RESEARCH RECOGNITIONCSMA PhD Thesis Award 2022

Selected among 28 PhD thesis applications and awarded in 2023 for research combining AI and physics-based modeling. Also nominated for the ECCOMAS PhD Thesis Award.

What sets me apart is the combination of industrial engineering and computational research. I understand the practical demands of aircraft development and can develop new modeling approaches to address them. I bring that perspective to engineering teams, connecting mechanical understanding, detailed analysis, and efficient simulation methods to support design decisions.
